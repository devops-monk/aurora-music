Word-synced lyrics

Lyrics that breathe with the song

Apple Music-style lyrics with word-level timing from BetterLyrics, LRCLIB and YouTube Music. Words fill as they're sung, distant lines soften, and duets split left and right. Click any line to jump to it.

Crossfade & gapless

Songs that hand over, not stop

The next track is already buffered before this one ends. Set a crossfade and the two overlap on equal-power curves, so the level never dips.

Equaliser

Seven bands, sixteen presets

Seven bands from 60 Hz to 14 kHz, with automatic headroom so boosts never clip. The curve you see is the curve you hear.

Listen Together

One song, every device, the same second

Start a party, share the six-character code, and up to five people hear the same thing in sync. Devices measure their clock offset like NTP and each computes where it should be, so a slow network can't pull anyone off the beat. It speaks BitChord's party protocol, so Android friends can join too.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no ads, no subscription and no tracking. Aurora Music is free software under the GPL-3.0, and the source is on GitHub.

Do I need a YouTube Music account?

No. You can search and play without signing in. Signing in with Google adds your library, liked songs and personalised home feed. Your session stays encrypted in your system keychain.

Where do downloads go?

To Music/Aurora Music in your home folder, each with its cover image beside it. Downloaded songs play from disk automatically, online or offline.

Can I listen together with BitChord users?

Yes. Aurora's Listen Together speaks the same party protocol as BitChord on Android, so anyone connected to the same party server shares the party.

Is it affiliated with YouTube or Apple?

No. Aurora Music is an independent project. It's styled after Apple Music's design language and plays from YouTube Music, but it is not endorsed by or connected to either company.
